> FA | Field Artillery
> ---|---

> Ltr | Letter
> MEW | Mobile Early Warning
> Mng | Morning
> Msg | Message
> Mtg | Meeting
> NCO | Noncommissioned officer
> _Nebelwerfer_ | Multiple rocket projector
> NUSA | Ninth U.S. Army
> O | Officer
