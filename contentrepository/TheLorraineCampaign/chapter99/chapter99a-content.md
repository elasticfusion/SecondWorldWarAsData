# Chapter 99: Test Event

On 15 September 1944, the 35th Infantry Division advanced toward the town of Grémecey, east of Nancy. Colonel Butler's 137th Infantry Regiment encountered stiff resistance from elements of the 15th Panzergrenadier Division defending the ridge line. After a brief artillery preparation, the regiment seized the high ground by nightfall, capturing 45 prisoners.[1]
